Wedding invitation

Upload the whole folder to your web host. To preview on your own computer, double-click
preview.bat (Windows) or preview.command (Mac); it starts a tiny local server and opens the page.
Before sharing the link: replace YOUR-DOMAIN in the og:image line of index.html so WhatsApp shows the preview picture (assets/share.jpg).

EDIT TEXT: content/details.txt  (every word on the page, as 'label: value' lines)
RSVP / GOOGLE SHEET: content/rsvp-config.txt  (sheet link, tab, form fields, columns). Setup steps: rsvp/SETUP.md
MUSIC: replace bgm/bgm1.mp3 and bgm/bgm2.mp3 with your own tracks (keep the names).
  bgm1 plays once on the first page; when it ends the GIF stops on the hands-touching frame
  (assets/intro-end.jpg) and, one second later, the invitation opens by itself with the page turn.
  bgm2 then plays once. Tip: make bgm1 about as long as the GIF (20 seconds).
BACKGROUND: replace assets/bg.jpg to change the picture behind the details.
